Mosquito Control

Mosquitoes spend the hot part of the day hiding in shrubs, under decks and in tall plants. We treat those resting spots so far fewer mosquitoes are waiting for you when you step outside.

  • Treatment of trees, shrubs, decks and shady areas
  • Regular visits all season long, so protection doesn’t wear off
  • We point out standing water that lets mosquitoes breed
  • One-time treatments for graduations, weddings and backyard parties

Tick Control

Ticks wait in long grass, leaf piles and the edges of wooded areas. Here in Minnesota, deer ticks can carry Lyme disease, so keeping them out of your yard matters.

  • Treatment of yard edges, tall grass, gardens and leaf litter
  • Helps protect children, grandchildren and pets
  • Simple tips to make your yard less friendly to ticks
  • Included in the same visit as mosquito control
